Game Rules
==========

The project currently follows the gameplay behavior implemented in the Flask
application and front-end controls.

Round basics
------------

- The player places a bet before cards are dealt
- The dealer receives one visible up card and one hidden hole card
- The player acts first on the active seat and active hand
- The dealer resolves after all player hands are complete, surrendered, or bust
- A natural blackjack is paid immediately at 3:2

Supported actions
-----------------

Hit
^^^

Draw one additional card for the active hand.

Stand
^^^^^

End play for the active hand and move to the next eligible hand or seat.

Double down
^^^^^^^^^^^

Double down is available on eligible hands before the hand has taken an
ordinary hit. The player receives exactly one more card and the hand then ends.

Split
^^^^^

When the active hand holds a splittable pair, the player can split into two
hands. Split hands are shown separately in the large hand panel.

Surrender
^^^^^^^^^

Surrender is available only when the current hand is eligible. A surrendered
hand is resolved immediately at half the bet loss.

Bankroll and rounds
-------------------

- The current implementation uses one bankroll shared across all selected seats
- Choosing ``Next Hand`` starts a fresh table setup flow while preserving the bankroll
- Gameplay state is session-based rather than account-based
